Why KelkuSuno

Kelku and Suno both mean listen

In Malayalam, kelku means listen. In Hindi, suno means listen too. Two languages, one wish.

Most of us are told what to do long before anyone has heard how we feel. Here, we begin the other way round. You speak, we listen, and we take our time together.

We will not promise you a solution. We do promise to listen with care, ask gentle questions and sit beside you while we explore what could help, one small step at a time. Sometimes talking is enough. Sometimes a next step appears. The choice is always yours.

A little about who we are

Who you will talk with

Talk with someone who has walked a path like yours. The people here are of different ages and from different walks of life.

Our team includes engineers, research scientists, industrial psychologists and peace practitioners. Some of our mentors and coaches are certified yoga wellness instructors (AYUSH), deep relaxation practitioners (My Vinyasa Practice, US) and certified life coaches (Transformation Services Inc).

Whoever you meet, we listen first, then help you think things through at your own pace. Tell us what you are looking for, and we will bring the right person to talk with you.

You are welcome to speak with us in English, Hindi or Malayalam.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. A Space to Talk is personal coaching, and Deep Relaxation with Talk is a wellness practice. Neither one diagnoses or treats anything, and neither replaces a doctor. If things feel very heavy, please see the help numbers at the bottom of this page.
